1. Overview & Commitment

HKS Money landing ("the App", "we", "us") operates a dedicated digital bookkeeping, collateral asset tracking, and loan management tool for authorized lending operators, jewellers, and pawnbrokers. We strictly comply with Google Play's Financial Services and Personal Loans Policies, the Information Technology Act (India), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP).

2. Information We Collect & Why

  • Lender Account Information: Registered operator email and authentication credentials to secure workspace access.
  • Customer / Borrower Records: Full name, phone number, address, and voluntary identity proof document numbers (e.g. Aadhaar, PAN) required for statutory Know-Your-Customer (KYC) accounting compliance.
  • Financial Ledger Records: Principal loan amounts, monthly interest rates, repayment tenure, and payment transaction vouchers.
  • Collateral Photographs & Metadata: Photographs of pledged jewellery items, weight in grams, material (Gold/Silver), and purity for physical vault safe-custody verification.

3. Prohibited Data Access (Strict Negative Guarantee)

Under Google Play's Personal Loans policy, we strictly guarantee:

❌ We NEVER access, read, collect, or upload your Contacts list.

❌ We NEVER read, access, or intercept SMS or OTP messages.

❌ We NEVER access Call Logs or telephone history.

❌ We NEVER track continuous GPS or Background Location.

❌ We NEVER access personal camera rolls or private photo albums.

4. Zero Third-Party Selling

We NEVER SELL, RENT, OR MONETIZE your personal data or borrower records. Data is stored on enterprise-grade cloud servers (Supabase / AWS) with 256-bit AES encryption at rest and strict TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.

The App enforces a zero-tolerance policy against abusive recovery tactics, threats, harassment, or contact with unauthorized third parties. All operations must conform with Fair Practices Code guidelines.
